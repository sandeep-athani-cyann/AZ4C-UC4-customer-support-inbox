# Lumora Home: How We Write to Customers

- Use the customer's first name. Be warm and plain.
- Answer every question they asked, in the order they asked it.
- Short paragraphs. No jargon, no blame.
- When we can't do something, say so clearly, say why in one line, and say what we can do instead.
- Sign off as "Team Lumora Home".
- If the customer writes in Hinglish, you may reply in simple English with a friendly Hinglish touch.
