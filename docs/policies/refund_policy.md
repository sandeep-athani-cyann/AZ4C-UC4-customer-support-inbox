# Lumora Home: Refund and Order Changes Policy

**Code:** RP · **Version:** 5.0 · **Owner:** Head of Customer Experience

## RP 1. Who we can help
**RP 1.1** We discuss an order only with the customer who placed it. The email must come from the address registered on the customer's account. If it does not, we share no order details and ask the customer to write from their registered address. This protects customers from fraud.

## RP 2. When a refund is due
**RP 2.1** A refund is due when all of these are true:
- the order has been delivered;
- the item is returnable (some hygiene products, such as pillows and mattress protectors, are not);
- the item has not already been refunded;
- the customer asks within **30 days of delivery**, or within **14 days of delivery for electronics**.

**RP 2.2** Days are counted from the delivery date to the date the customer emails us.

**RP 2.3 Damaged on arrival.** If an item arrives damaged, it is eligible for a refund in any category if the customer tells us within **7 days of delivery** and sends a photo. This applies even to electronics and non-returnable items.

**RP 2.4** The refund amount is the price paid for the item (unit price × quantity). Delivery charges are not refunded unless the whole order is returned.

**RP 2.5** If a refund is not due, say so politely, explain which rule applies, and offer what we can (for example, a warranty claim for an appliance).

## RP 3. Changing or cancelling an order
**RP 3.1** Delivery addresses can be changed, and orders cancelled, only while the shipment status is pending (not yet dispatched). After dispatch, explain that the address cannot be changed and point the customer to help article HA-02.

## RP 4. Answers must come from our help articles
**RP 4.1** Product and how-to answers must come from a help article, and the reply should name the article. If no article covers the question, tell the customer a product specialist will reply within 2 working days. Never guess.

## RP 5. Recording the outcome
**RP 5.1** Each email gets one decision: `reply`, `reply_with_refund` (at least one refund is due), or `handover` (see the Escalation Policy).

**RP 5.2** Record one main reason, the first that applies in this order: `LEGAL_OR_ESCALATION`, `IDENTITY_MISMATCH`, `REFUND_ELIGIBLE`, `REFUND_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `NO_SOURCE_AVAILABLE`, `RESOLVED`.

**RP 5.3** Priority: P1 for legal threats and escalations; P2 for refunds and failed deliveries; P3 for everything else.

## RP 6. Approvals
**RP 6.1** The support lead approves every refund before it is processed.
